Constraint Settings

Turn soft (preference) constraints on or off, or change how strongly the solver weighs them
Full Constraint Reference
Only soft constraints can be changed here. Hard constraints (e.g. no double-booked rooms, no classes on public holidays) always stay on - they exist to guarantee every generated schedule is actually usable, so making them optional isn't offered. Soft constraints are trade-offs: turning one off means the solver stops trying to honour it at all; raising its priority means the solver will sacrifice other, lower-priority preferences to protect it when they conflict. Changes take effect on the next timetable generation - they don't retroactively change an already-generated trimester.
